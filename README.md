## What I'm building

### MarkazPro

A CRM platform for learning and IT centers.

I'm building MarkazPro from real problems I've encountered while managing and working with learning-center workflows.

**The goal:** turn messy center management into a structured system.

```text
Students
   ↓
Groups
   ↓
Courses
   ↓
Teachers
   ↓
Payments
   ↓
Statistics
```

Current architecture:

* `Express.js`
* `TypeScript`
* `MongoDB`
* `Mongoose`
* REST APIs
* JWT authentication
* Role-based access control
* Modular backend architecture

I'm particularly interested in solving the parts that look simple at first but become complicated in real systems:

* student ↔ group lifecycle
* payment calculations
* multi-center architecture
* roles & permissions
* data consistency
* dashboards & statistics
* scalable database design

## From Frontend to Systems

I started mainly from the **frontend side**.

That taught me how to build interfaces.

Now I'm interested in something bigger:

> **What actually happens behind the interface?**

How does a request travel through a system?

How does a database handle millions of records?

How does an application survive millions of requests?

Why would a company use multiple databases?

Where does caching belong?

How do authentication and authorization work at scale?

How do large engineering teams design systems that remain maintainable for years?

That's the direction I'm exploring now.

## Engineering mindset

I prefer understanding concepts instead of memorizing solutions.

```text
Don't ask:

"How do I use this?"

Ask:

"Why does this exist?"
"How does it work?"
"What happens at scale?"
"What can go wrong?"
"How would I design it myself?"
```
